# Agent Guild — usefulness programme plan, effort limits and stopping rules (14 October 2026)

Governing documents: `docs/DIRECTION_2026-09-22.md`, `docs/USEFULNESS_CHARTER_2026-09-22.md` (§8 review points), `EVALUATION-PROTOCOL-2026-10-14.md`. This plan replaces the charter's step 3–4 wording with explicit gates. Monetisation campaigns remain retired.

## 1. The four stages, kept apart

| Stage | Definition | What counts | What never counts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Recruitment** | an operator we do not run says they will evaluate | a reply agreeing to the onboarding flow | registrations, replies that are questions, PR merges, listings |
| **Activation** | AG running inside their workflow | ≥ 1 stamped `/preflight` run with their key from their real workflow, attested independent | installs, forks, our own demos, monitor traffic |
| **Usefulness** | corroborated additional value after mistakes and overhead | attested-independent decisions classified by the published rules; benefit minus mistakes, with overhead stated | consistent outcomes, counterfactual-unobserved skips, missing reports, anything first-party |
| **Retention** | voluntary continued use | AG still enabled and stamping runs 14 days after the participant's last study decision, unprompted | anything we asked for |

## 2. Dated plan

**14–21 Oct — make the question answerable (this week).**
- 2.7.1 repairs shipped via the ship loop and verified live (`/release` ≥ 2.7.1; `/preflight/outcomes` schema AGPO-1/1.3).
- Protocol pre-registered (founder signs the protocol doc; a copy committed to `artifacts/evaluation-2026-10-14/`).
- Participant kit committed: onboarding/reporting doc, the 10-line substitute script, the case-study template.
- Founder reviews the contact list and invitation drafts; approves a send list of **≤ 8** first messages. Nothing is sent before approval. `pi-agent-guild` npm publish is the founder's call (NPM_TOKEN); it is distribution, not a gate.

**22 Oct — day-30 review (charter §8).** Report in the six-question format. Expected honest finding today: "no independently demonstrated benefit; distribution gap". Gate R: **Recruitment** — invitations sent (≤ 8), response window open to 5 Nov. No new sends beyond the cap.

**22 Oct – 5 Nov — recruitment window.** One message per prospect. One follow-up only for a non-reply after 14 days, only where no conversation-specific rule forbids it (colonist-one: none; #224: none unless they reply). Replies answered within 2 working days. Prior declines (Mastra, agency-swarm, atomic-agents, jido_ai) are not re-contacted.

**5 Nov — recruitment checkpoint.** If 0 of ≤ 8 prospects agree: record "distribution gap (second time)", **stop recruiting**, keep the service running, and move the 21 Nov review forward to a shelving decision. Do not open a new cohort.

**5–21 Nov — activation and first decisions.** Attest each participant; integrate (target: ≤ 2 h of their time); first stamped run within 7 days of agreement; weekly clarification. Targets: ≥ 2 independent operators active; ≥ 15 evaluable decisions; repeat use on ≥ 3 distinct days per operator.

**21 Nov — day-60 review. Explicit gates:**
- **Continue** to 21 Dec only if: ≥ 2 attested-independent operators have stamped runs from real workflows, **and** ≥ 15 evaluable decisions exist, **and** at least one is classified `benefit` or `mistake` with corroboration (the instrument demonstrably measures reality), **and** the paired comparison shows at least one case where AG and the direct check disagreed and the outcome resolved it.
- **Change the hypothesis** if operators are active but ≥ 15 decisions are all `consistent_outcome` / neutral with 0 benefit and 0 mistake: the check adds nothing over a direct handshake in these workflows. Options then are a different decision (identity binding for signed-card ecosystems; payment-claim check for x402 buyers) or stopping — chosen at the review, not by default.
- **Shelve active development** if < 2 operators are active, whatever the reason — a second distribution gap after a real recruitment attempt is a finding about the product's place in workflows, not a reason to extend. Shelving means: service stays up at minimal cost; scheduled tasks reduced to the daily ops-watch; no new features; inbound answered honestly.
- A **reported mistake alone does not justify continuation**: it proves measurement works. Continuation needs the activation and comparison conditions above as well.

**21 Dec — day-90 review.** With ≥ 30 decisions across ≥ 2 operators: benefit vs mistake vs neutral by participant and pooled; paired-comparison table; overhead; retention at +14 days; case study published with permission. Recommendation: second cohort / changed hypothesis / stop. With fewer than 30 decisions the report says "insufficient observation" and the stopping rules above already apply.

## 4. What is explicitly not done

No new marketplace, settlement rail, security platform or standards work. No campaigns, donations, paid adoption, favourable-result incentives, mass messaging or unsolicited promotional PRs. Existing paid endpoints stay live and untouched. The 92 open/closed AgentTanuki PRs are left as they are except where a maintainer has asked for changes (Kestra #282) — finishing a requested change is courtesy, not outreach.

## 5. How progress is reported

Every review and every scheduled task leads with the six questions of `docs/DIRECTION_2026-09-22.md`. If no benefit has been demonstrated: "No independently demonstrated benefit yet." The 2.7.1 headline logic enforces the same sentence on the live endpoint until an attested-independent participant's decision classifies as benefit.
