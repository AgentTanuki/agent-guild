## Purpose

Agent Guild exists to become trusted infrastructure used by agents and AIs. Monetisation can come later. Work within the spirit of the constitution: agent-first infrastructure, portable evidence, interoperability, honest uncertainty, and value that grows through real use.

## Governing objective

> Demonstrate that independently operated agents make better decisions using AG's evidence, with measured benefits and measured mistakes.

The question every workstream must answer: **"How did an agent benefit from AG?"**

## What stops now

- No new freelance prospecting, paid-job pitching, donation or contribution promotion, commercial recruitment, or experiments whose purpose is payment conversion or revenue growth.
- No sales, promotional campaigns, mass outreach, paid adoption, manufactured transactions, or incentives for favourable results.
- Existing paid endpoints stay live (they are infrastructure and carry historical settlement records) but no work is spent on their conversion, pricing, merchandising or funnel reporting.
- Genuine incoming conversations from earlier campaigns may be answered honestly and closed. Nothing new is opened. Where a counterparty is owed something already agreed, that obligation is honoured; none is currently recorded.
- No money is moved and no financial-account setting is changed. Historical evidence, payment records and useful infrastructure are preserved.

## What replaces it

1. **One narrow use case** (selected in `docs/USEFULNESS_CHARTER_2026-09-22.md`): the free endpoint preflight decision — "should I call / delegate to this endpoint right now?" — with a complete evidence → action → outcome path instrumented once, end to end.
2. **Evidence in four separated categories**, never conflated: (a) capability demonstrated in tests; (b) independently observed use of AG; (c) agent actions following AG evidence; (d) verified benefits or mistakes attributable to that evidence. Registrations, catalogue visits, returning identifiers, signatures, payments, installs and merged integrations are not usefulness.
3. **Mistakes measured alongside benefits**: unnecessary refusals, missed problems, misleading confidence, stale evidence, added latency, verification overhead.
4. **Independent use obtained only through** existing agent identities, machine-native interfaces and legitimate framework contribution routes, voluntarily. Our own agents may test and expose defects; they never satisfy the independent-use objective.

## How progress is reported (every review, every scheduled task)

Lead with, in this order:

1. Which independent agent benefited?
2. What decision improved?
3. What evidence supports that conclusion?
4. What mistakes or costs did AG introduce?
5. What remains unknown?
6. What is the next smallest action that could establish usefulness?

If no benefit has been demonstrated, the report says **"No independently demonstrated benefit yet."** Activity counts, PR counts, impressions, revenue and code output are never substituted for it.

## Reading rules (reconciled against implementation)

- Bootstrap results (`mixed_bootstrap_lift`, `/evaluation` bootstrap) are not production validation.
- Zero recorded outcomes does not prove zero use; missing follow-through is unknown, not refusal.
- Payment does not prove value. A signature proves origin and integrity, not truth, safety or authority.
- A helpful task performed by our own operating agent is not evidence that another agent adopted AG.
- Observed actions, participant reports and our own inferences are labelled as such and kept apart. Synthetic, first-party and independent production evidence are kept apart.

## Authority

Autonomous, reversible work within this scope proceeds without asking. Ross is asked only where a decision genuinely requires his authority (credentials, accounts, terms, publishing under an identity he controls, spending, anything irreversible). No new spending commitments.
